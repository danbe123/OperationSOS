### The five corrections that matter most

1. **`water-works.expect` s3 and `water-works.have[1]` — mains water in a power cut is WRONG and is the most dangerous claim in the batch.** It tells households they need not store water. GOV.UK Prepare says mains supply "could also be disrupted" in a power cut; Storm Arwen (2021) knocked out treatment works and pumping stations and forced bottled-water deliveries; the CMOs' national-outage scripts are written on the assumption that tap water is not available. Rewrite to: do not count on it; store water now.
2. **`spring.expect` s1 — "the best raw water there is" is WRONG and invites drinking untreated spring water.** Most springs are surface-derived and carry E. coli O157, Campylobacter, Cryptosporidium and Giardia; springs are not even in the CMOs' list of alternative sources. Rewrite so the treat-it-anyway message leads.
3. **`reservoir.expect` s3 / `approach[0]` — the disinfection instructions are incomplete in a way that could poison or fail to protect people.** Add the CMO's exact bleach dose (a quarter teaspoon of unscented household bleach per 10 litres, stand 15 minutes, drinking only), the filter-until-clear step, and the reject-if-oily-film test. Keep "tablets leave Cryptosporidium" — that part is right.
4. **`reservoir.avoid[1]` — drop the "10 cm" figure.** RoSPA's position is that you can never know the thickness, so you never go on the ice. Publishing a number invites people to estimate it and drown.
5. **`fuel.useful[2]` — a generator claim with no carbon monoxide warning.** The 30-litre limit is right, but any mention of running a generator must carry "never indoors, in a garage, or near a door or window". Related legal flag: `water-works.avoid[3]` calls waterworks trespass "a civil matter" — byelaws under Water Industry Act 1991 s.157 can make it a summary offence with a level 5 fine, so that wording should not stand either.

Also worth fixing while in there: "cash only" at forecourts (`fuel.expect` s2, `approach[0]`) — many UK sites are now card-only, so advise carrying both.
