Source of truth for this round: the four independent fact-checker reports in this folder —
`health-report.md`, `utilities-report.md`, `hazards-report.md`, `land-report.md` — read in full,
against the claims tagged in `*-claims.md`.

Every WRONG, PARTLY and UNSUPPORTED verdict was actioned. VERIFIED claims were left alone except
where a report asked for an addition (a 999 instruction, a nation-specific detail) or where a
mandatory safety flag applied.

Changed in `playbooks/map/places.yaml` (and mirrored verbatim, with the fixture guide slugs, into
`api/tests/fixtures/playbooks/map/places.yaml`):

- 16 `expect` paragraphs rewritten (every kind)
- 134 bullets reworded
- 2 bullets deleted (`hospital.have[4]`, `hospital.have[5]`)
- 1 bullet added (`hospital.avoid[4]`)
- 20 guide sentences and table rows corrected across 11 guides (15 sentences of prose, 5 table
  rows), plus 2 `sources:` entries and 1 card `## Source` citation added

All lists stay inside the schema counts (have 3–7, useful 2–5, avoid 2–5, approach 2–6); every
bullet is 6–30 words and 20–220 characters; every `expect` is 40–120 words.

  of a teaspoon of unscented household bleach per 10 litres, stand 15 minutes" on
  `reservoir.expect[s3]`. `playbooks/pages/water-disinfection.md`, the guide the card links to,

  `playbooks/README.md` requires every figure on a map card to be one the linked guide already
  holds. Publishing two different doses on the same box would be worse than publishing one, so the
  card now says "at the doses on the disinfection page ... boil whenever you can" and the dose stays
  in one place. Reconciling the two UK doses is a separate decision for the content owner.
- **No `sources:` list was added to any page or card.** `api/tests/test_playbooks_content.py` fixes
  the front-matter key set for pages (`id, title, icon, order, summary, category`) and cards
  (`id, title, icon, order, summary`), so only modules can carry `sources:`. Primary sources for the
  page and card corrections were therefore cited inline in the body, or in the card's `## Source`
  section, which is how the rest of the tree cites them. Sources were added to the `sources:` lists
  of `modules/water.md` and `modules/evacuation.md`, which do carry them.
- **The 48–72 hour hospital generator fuel figure was not published.** health-report.md gives it for
  `hospital.have[0]`, but no guide in the tree holds it and the underlying source (HTM/SHTM 06-01
  derogations) is not in the offline manifest, so the bullet and the what-still-works row say the
  fuel is finite and needs resupply without quoting a number.
- **The one-minute rolling boil was kept.** utilities-report.md notes the one-minute hold is
  WHO/CDC rather than UK-stated, but marks it PARTLY and safe; it is the figure
  `playbooks/pages/water-disinfection.md` already carries, so it stays.
- **No content test needed updating.** `api/tests/test_playbooks_content.py` asserts structure,
  front matter and a handful of numbers (emergency numbers, PMR446 channels), none of which a report
  found wrong, so no assertion was changed.
