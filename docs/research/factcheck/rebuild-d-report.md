## The five most important corrections

1. **Burial distances from water (`rebuild-first-year`).** "As far as the ground allows" is
   replaced by the Environment Agency's binding figures: **250 m** from any well, borehole or
   spring used for drinking water, **30 m** from any spring or watercourse, **10 m** from a
   field drain or ditch, and **1 m** of dry ground above the water table. The current wording,
   read next to the page's own 30 m latrine rule, invites a grave eight times too close to a
   well. `death-and-grief` should get the same numbers — it currently says only that the EA
   figures are larger and are not in the library.

2. **"Rain off a clean sheet, which needs no treatment" (`rebuild-essentials-printed`).** CDC:
   *"Rainwater is not necessarily safe to drink without first removing germs and chemicals from
   it."* This is the printed sheet, so there is no table to check it against, and
   `water-disinfection` already says the opposite. Fix here **and** in `fieldcraft-water` line 17.

3. **"Never remove the first pad" and "press for at least 10 minutes" (`rebuild-essentials-printed`
   and `card:severe-bleeding`).** SJA now says *"remove it and reapply pressure with a new
   dressing"*; the 10-minute rule belongs to nosebleeds, and waiting it out delays escalation to
   packing or a tourniquet in catastrophic haemorrhage. Both errors are in the shipped card too.

4. **The improvised filter "takes out parasites" (`rebuild-essentials-printed`).** That property
   belongs to the 0.1–0.2 micron row of the box's own table, not to cloth or a fresh gravel bed —
   a schmutzdecke takes weeks to months to form. As printed it tells a reader that filtered water
   is parasite-free, which is exactly the Cryptosporidium failure the rest of the page guards against.

5. **The householder self-defence test and the CCA "only lawful route" (`rebuild-law-and-trade`).**
   *R v Ray* is explicit that force short of grossly disproportionate must still be reasonable, and
   the householder provision does not cover property or the garden — the current wording could
   encourage force that ends in a conviction. Separately, the CCA is not the only route (the 2020
   lockdowns used PHCDA 1984 s.45C) and regulations need both Houses' approval within **7 days**,
   which this page omits though `security-law` states it correctly.

**Runners-up worth fixing in the same pass:** the quicklime slaking ratio (3.1 kg/litre is the

give litres of water per kilogram of lime instead, and 3.6 MJ rather than 3.54); the soap section,
which tells the reader to cut a bar out of potash lye that can only make soft soap; and the LASPO
fine, which has been unlimited rather than £5,000 since 12 March 2015.
