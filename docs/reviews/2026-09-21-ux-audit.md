# Cross-screen UX audit, 2026-09-21

Branch `ux-audit`, from main at `bdffc7f`. Every screen in the fixture-mode browser suite (`pnpm e2e`,
port 4173) was visited at the kiosk's own 853x480 and at a phone's 390x844, in the `field` theme, plus
a second pass in dim mode and a portrait-phone pass of the on-screen keyboard. The reused scratch
harness `web/e2e/zz-audit.spec.ts` (left from an interrupted earlier attempt on this branch, and kept —
it already covered essentially every route, both viewports and dim mode) drove 114 route/viewport
combinations, took a screenshot of each, and measured touch-target size (< 44px, WCAG's own floor;
the project's own spec at `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-03-operation-sos-design.md` asks for 48px),
text clipped by its own box, content pushed off the right or left edge, and colour contrast under
7:1/4.5:1/3:1 in every theme. Screenshots were then read and looked at, not just measured. Search
screens (`web/src/screens/Search*.tsx`, `api/sos/search.py`) were **not** touched — another agent was
changing Books grouping there concurrently — so Search issues below are reported only.

Every screen in the app's route table was visited at both viewports (Now in four states, every
scenario tab, every module/page/quick card, the dose calculator, Library and all four shelves, the
book and document readers including a missing book/doc, the map and its layers/home panels, Find in
three states, notes and pins, the situation sheet, tasks, the board (drill and peacetime), System, the
AI screen off, a 404, and dim mode on six of those). Not captured: the true no-results Find state (the
fixture backend has no way to return zero rows — see Search notes below), Connect-a-phone/QR states,
the drill debrief dialog, and the print stylesheet — these are gaps in this pass, not screens found and
skipped.

## Checked and found clean

Touch targets across the rest of the app (rail/bar destinations, scenario tiles, checklist rows, kit
rows, quick-card links, timer buttons, board condition/job buttons, the theme button); colour contrast
in `field` and `mono`, including dim mode, in every screen visited (the harness's automated 7:1/4.5:1
ratio check raised zero findings); horizontal scroll/offscreen content (none, outside the map's
deliberately-scrolling toolbar and chip strip); focus order and visible focus ring (spot-checked on the
children's-doses age field); empty/loading/error states read in plain words with no jargon ("The
assistant is off", "Could not open this book: Book not found", "999 will not connect while both
networks are down", "Nothing to do.", "No pins yet — drop one from the map."); back navigation present
and consistent on every screen that should have one (already covered by `web/e2e/ux.spec.ts`); dim
mode is a deliberately subtle token shift in `field` (by design, per `tokens.css`'s own comment) and a
near-black `mono`, not a dramatic overlay — screenshots that "look the same" between normal and dim
`field` are working as designed, not a miss; the reconnecting strip and its interaction with the band,
keyboard and multiple screens is already covered by the existing `web/e2e/reconnect.spec.ts` (11
scenarios, all passing).

## Method notes

- Fixture-mode Playwright suite, `LD_LIBRARY_PATH=$HOME/.local/chromium-deps/usr/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu`,
  one worker at a time (11GB RAM under WSL).
- `web/e2e/zz-audit.spec.ts` is a scratch measurement harness (screenshots + heuristics), not part of
  the ordinary suite; it is left untracked/uncommitted per the task's instructions to keep it out of
  the branch, and can be deleted once this report is read.
- Severity: **blocks use** (a task cannot be completed, or critical information is unreachable),
  **annoying** (works, but harder or less certain than it should be — fixed here when the fix was
  small and low-risk, left for the owner when it touches a documented design trade-off),
  **polish** (cosmetic, no functional effect).
