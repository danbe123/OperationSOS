## Right now

**Survive the first month with the playbook for the cause**, then come back here. The register's own words on recovery: it "can last months, years or even decades" and works best when the affected community takes part in deciding how ([NRR 2025, p. 22](doc:nrr-2025#page=22)). Every household action it recommends for the first days still applies: water, food, warmth, a radio, neighbours ([NRR 2025, p. 21](doc:nrr-2025#page=21)).

**Order of work:** shelter and water together, then sanitation, then food, then security, then everything else. In a British winter exposure kills before thirst does, which is why the survival manual puts shelter ahead of food and sometimes ahead of water ([FM 21-76, p. 38](doc:fm-21-76-survival#page=38)); cholera and typhoid, not violence, emptied cities that lost their sewers ([Cholera (WikiMed)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_medicine_maxi/Cholera); [Sanitation module](module:sanitation)).

**Take stock of people.** Skills, tools, animals, land, seed, medicines, fuel, and who needs care: the community plan's asset and skills registers, on paper, in the hall ([Community module](module:community)).

## First 72 hours

- **Water for the street.** A well, a spring or a stream with a settling tank, a cloth filter and chlorine or boiling; a hand pump on a borehole is the long-term answer ([Well](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Well); [Hand pump](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Hand_pump); [Water Treatment Library](kiwix:zimgit-water_en/home)).
- **Latrines** at least 30 metres from any water and downhill of it, or composting toilets ([Sanitation module](module:sanitation); [Approved Document H, p. 31](doc:ad-h#page=31)). Burial has its own, longer distances: at least 250 m from any well, borehole or spring used for drinking water, 30 m from any other spring or watercourse, 10 m from a field drain, no standing water in the grave, and the death registered first ([Death and grief](page:death-and-grief); [After a death](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/after-a-death)).
- **Food.** Ration what is stored, sow what the month allows, and organise the animals ([Food module](module:food); [Famine playbook](playbook:famine)). What 2,000 kcal a day looks like, and how to keep grain and flour in bulk, is on [Food storage](page:food-storage); killing and dressing animals is on [Butchery](page:butchery).
- **Security without weapons:** locks, a dog, a watch rota, agreements with neighbours, no displays of plenty; the law of theft and reasonable force does not lapse because the police cannot come ([Security and the law module](module:security-law)).
- **Records.** A ledger of who has what, who owes what, who did what; disputes in year three are settled by the book written in week one ([Community module](module:community)).

## First month

- **Medicine without the NHS.** The village handbook is Where There Is No Doctor ([Where There Is No Doctor](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor)); austere medicine, suturing, dental extraction and childbirth are in Survival and Austere Medicine ([Survival and Austere Medicine, p. 10](doc:survival-austere-medicine-2017#page=10)); the WHO essential list is the pharmacy to build ([WHO EML, p. 12](doc:who-eml-2025#page=12)). Antibiotics expire slowly; tetanus, typhoid and childbirth are the killers to plan for ([Tetanus (NHS)](kiwix:nhs_uk/www.nhs.uk/conditions/tetanus/); [Typhoid fever (NHS)](kiwix:nhs_uk/www.nhs.uk/conditions/typhoid-fever/); [Childbirth card](card:childbirth)).
- **Power.** Salvaged panels, a wind turbine, a water wheel or micro-hydro on a stream, lead-acid or lithium batteries kept above half, and 12 V everything ([Micro hydro](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Micro_hydro); [Lead–acid battery](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Lead–acid_battery); [Power module](module:power)). This box on a panel and a battery is the library.
- **Tools and repair.** Hand tools, the spares list, the knowledge to fix rather than replace ([Tools and repair module](module:tools-repair); [iFixit](kiwix:ifixit_en_all/home/home)); the Home Improvement and Woodworking Q&A for the questions that arise ([Home Improvement Q&A](kiwix:diy.stackexchange.com_en_all/questions); [Woodworking Q&A](kiwix:woodworking.stackexchange.com_en_all/questions)).
- **Fuel.** Coppice woodland cut on rotation gives firewood for ever, and the wood is cut a year ahead to dry; charcoal for the forge ([Coppicing](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Coppicing); [Charcoal](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Charcoal)).
- **Navigation and range.** Footpaths, byways and the old roads; bicycles and carts; a grid reference for every place that matters ([Navigation module](module:navigation); [footpaths overlay](map:?overlay=footpaths&overlay=access-land)).

## Long term

**Year one to three: food and health.** The rotation, the fertility without bought fertiliser and the seed-saving are in the [growing food module](module:growing-food) below; what a village adds is scale and a second staple. Potatoes alone are the Irish lesson, so a grain crop and a mill come early: wheat, oats or barley on the best ground, dried hard, stored in bulk away from mice and damp, and milled as needed ([Gristmill](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Gristmill); [Food storage](page:food-storage)). Animals are killed and butchered at home ([Butchery](page:butchery)); the surplus is preserved by drying, salting, smoking and fermenting ([Food preservation](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Food_preservation); [Food drying](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Food_drying); [Sauerkraut](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Sauerkraut)); a sourdough starter, a brew and a cheese vat turn grain and milk into food that keeps ([Sourdough](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Sourdough); [Cheesemaking](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Cheesemaking); [Homebrewing Q&A](kiwix:homebrew.stackexchange.com_en_all/questions)); bees give the only sweetener and the wax for candles ([Beekeeping](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Beekeeping)). Midwifery, vaccination if any vaccine survives, and clean water keep children alive ([Midwifery](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Midwifery); [Vaccination](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Vaccination)).

**Year three to ten: trades and materials**, each with its first step. Soap: leach lye from wood ash through straw, boil it with rendered fat, pour and cure ([Soap](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Soap); [Lye](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Lye)). Candles: render tallow or melt beeswax, dip a wick again and again ([Candle](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Candle)). Lime: burn chalk or limestone in a kiln, slake the quicklime carefully with water, mix with sand for mortar and plaster ([Lime kiln](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Lime_kiln); [Lime mortar](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Lime_mortar)). Buildings: green oak pegged into frames, subsoil and straw trodden into cob, reed or straw thatched, always on a structure that meets the load rules ([Timber framing](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Timber_framing); [Cob (material)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Cob_(material)); [Thatching](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Thatching); [Approved Document A](doc:ad-a)). Iron: a blacksmith working scrap steel on a charcoal forge comes years before a bloomery smelting ore ([Blacksmith](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Blacksmith); [Bloomery](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Bloomery)). Leather and cloth: hides soaked in oak-bark liquor for months, fleece washed and carded then spun and woven ([Tanning (leather)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Tanning_(leather)); [Spinning wheel](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Spinning_wheel); [Loom](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Loom)). Paper from pulped rag, salt from boiled sea water ([Papermaking](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Papermaking); [Sea salt](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Sea_salt)). Mills on water and wind, first for grain and then for sawing and hammering ([Watermill](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Watermill); [Windmill](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Windmill)). Horses and carts on the old roads, and the canals, which were built for exactly this ([Draft horse](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Draft_horse); [Canals of the United Kingdom](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Canals_of_the_United_Kingdom)). The appropriate-technology wiki and the engineering Q&A carry the detail ([Appropedia](kiwix:appropedia_en_all/Welcome_to_Appropedia); [Engineering Q&A](kiwix:engineering.stackexchange.com_en_all/questions)); the book this playbook is named after lists what to rebuild first ([The Knowledge](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/The_Knowledge:_How_to_Rebuild_Our_World_from_Scratch)).

**Law and community.** Britain after Rome lost coinage and pottery within a generation ([Fall of the Western Roman Empire](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Fall_of_the_Western_Roman_Empire)); the Black Death killed a third to a half and the survivors renegotiated everything ([Black Death in England](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Black_Death_in_England)); disease without the NHS is the [pandemic playbook](playbook:pandemic). Collapses are usually slow and partial, and what carries people through is the parish: a council that meets, a ledger, a common law of contract and theft that everyone still recognises, and mutual aid ([Societal collapse](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Societal_collapse); [Parish council (England)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Parish_council_(England)); [Common law](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Common_law); [Mutual aid](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Mutual_aid)). Barter and commodity money return before paper does ([Commodity money](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Commodity_money)).

## UK specifics

- **What Britain has.** Rain, grass, coppice woodland, coal and peat, tidal and running water, a long coast, the most detailed maps in the world and a dense network of footpaths and old roads ([footpaths overlay](map:?overlay=footpaths&overlay=access-land&overlay=rail); [Navigation module](module:navigation)). Reservoirs and water works are marked ([water overlay](map:?overlay=water)).
- **What Britain lacks.** A short growing season in the north, thin soils on the uplands, a population far above what its farmland fed before the Haber process ([Haber process](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Haber_process)); food is the constraint that decides how many people a valley holds ([Famine playbook](playbook:famine)).
- **Law that remains.** The Civil Contingencies Act's emergency regulations while any government functions ([Civil Contingencies Act 2004](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/36/contents)); the offences of theft, assault and criminal damage; reasonable force ([CJIA 2008 s76](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2008/4/section/76)); the weapons law that keeps a village from arming itself against itself ([Knife and firearms law](page:knife-firearms-law)).
- **The parish** is the institution with the longest record: councils in England, community councils in Scotland and Wales, with the hall, the noticeboard and the register of who needs help ([Parish council (England)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Parish_council_(England)); [Community council](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Community_council); [Ready Scotland community planning](kiwix:govuk_resilience/ready.campaign.gov.scot/community-emergency-planning)).
- **Building** to the Approved Documents where you can, because they are the distilled reasons houses fall down, burn and poison people: [Approved Document A](doc:ad-a) for how much a wall, beam or roof will carry; [Approved Document B volume 1](doc:ad-b1) for escape routes and stopping fire spreading between houses; [Approved Document H](doc:ad-h) for drains, septic tanks and drainage fields; [Approved Document J](doc:ad-j) for flues, air supply, carbon monoxide alarms and oil tanks; [Approved Document L volume 1](doc:ad-l1) for how much insulation a loft, wall and window need; [Approved Document P](doc:ad-p) for what electrical work is safe to do yourself.
- **This box** on a panel, a battery and a spare drive in a tin is the library the village rebuilds from; keep a second copy somewhere dry ([About Operation SOS](page:about-sos)).

## Checklist

- [ ] The cause's playbook worked through for the first month before this one starts {#first-month-done now}
- [ ] Shelter and water together: the street's water source found, tested, filtered and disinfected; a hand pump planned {#street-water now}
- [ ] Latrines dug 30 metres from water and downhill, before anyone is ill; burial ground sited to the burial distances {#latrines-sited now}
- [ ] Skills, tools, animals, land, seed and medicines registered on paper in the hall {#asset-register now}
- [ ] Watch rota, locks and agreements with neighbours; no weapons {#security-rota today}
- [ ] Ledger started: who has what, who owes what, decisions taken {#ledger today}
- [ ] Enough ground under cultivation to feed everyone the valley holds; a grain crop and a mill planned; second year's seed in store {#food-year-one week}
- [ ] Animals kept and bred: hens, rabbits, goats, bees; hay for winter {#animals-bred week}
- [ ] Coppice cut on rotation; a year of firewood drying {#coppice week}
- [ ] Panels, turbine or water wheel and a battery keeping this box and the lights on {#village-power week}
- [ ] Medical kit built to the WHO essential list; a midwife and a first-aider trained {#village-medicine week}
- [ ] Parish or community council meeting monthly with minutes {#council-meets week}
- [ ] Second copy of this box's drive stored dry in a tin {#spare-library week}
