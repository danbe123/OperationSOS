## Right now

**Harvest everything that can be harvested, store it as if it must last two years, and set seed aside before anyone eats it** ([Growing food module](module:growing-food); [Food storage](page:food-storage)). Whatever caused it, the first month is the playbook for the cause: [nuclear war](playbook:nuclear-war), [volcanic ash](playbook:volcanic) or [severe winter](playbook:severe-winter); this one starts when the sky stays dim.

**Fuel and insulation before the first winter.** One warm room now ([Shelter and heat module](module:shelter-heat)); wood cut this year for next, because green wood will not burn well and there will be no other delivery; then the rest of the house brought up to the current insulation standard as materials allow ([Approved Document L volume 1](doc:ad-l1)).

**The signs it is happening:** a sun that stays dim and red for weeks, frosts in the wrong months and a failed harvest, because soot, sulphur or dust lofted high enough to stay for years cuts the light that crops need ([Nuclear winter](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Nuclear_winter); [Impact winter](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Impact_winter)). Tambora's 1815 eruption gave 1816 frosts in July and famine across Europe ([Year Without a Summer](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Year_Without_a_Summer)); the dimming of 536 brought eighteen months without proper sun ([Volcanic winter of 536](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Volcanic_winter_of_536)). A household that prepared for the cold and got a mild year lost nothing.

## First 72 hours

- **Fuel.** A winter that lasts years is heated with wood, and wood is cut a year ahead to dry; the coppice rotation, charcoal and the stove rules are in the [long rebuild playbook](playbook:long-rebuild) and the [shelter and heat module](module:shelter-heat) above. Heating oil, LPG and diesel while deliveries last, stored lawfully as in the [severe winter playbook](playbook:severe-winter) ([Approved Document J, p. 65](doc:ad-j#page=65)).
- **Insulation.** One warm room now; then the whole house to the current standard as materials allow: loft, walls, floors, windows ([Approved Document L volume 1](doc:ad-l1); [Building insulation](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Building_insulation)).
- **Water** freezes: {{#if water}}lag and bury pipes, know the stopcock, and store water indoors while the mains still runs{{else}}the mains has gone, so melt snow and ice indoors and boil it before drinking{{/if}} ([Water module](module:water); [Severe winter playbook](playbook:severe-winter)).
- **Power.** Solar panels give a fraction of their output under a dimmed sky ([Global dimming](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Global_dimming)); the sources that do not care about light, wind and running water, are in the [long rebuild playbook](playbook:long-rebuild) and the [power module](module:power) below.
- **Animals.** Cull early the herd you cannot feed through a summer without grass: an animal killed in autumn is meat and hide, one that starves in February is a loss; keep the few that live on scraps and hay ([Culling](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Culling); [Butchery](page:butchery); [Livestock module](module:livestock)).
- **Warm hubs.** While the council and the hall run, a warm hub with a generator is where the frail spend the day; how to find one is in the [severe winter playbook](playbook:severe-winter).
- **The register's** nearest scenarios are a nuclear war elsewhere with global economic and migration effects ([NRR 2025, p. 185](doc:nrr-2025#page=185)) and a week of deep snow ([NRR 2025, p. 143](doc:nrr-2025#page=143)); a years-long winter is beyond it.

## First month

- **Grow under light, not only under cover.** Cloches, tunnels, sprouting jars and mushroom beds are in the [growing food module](module:growing-food) below; what is new is that daylight itself is short, so where there is any power, LED grow lights over trays of salad, sprouts and seedlings turn electricity into food when the sky will not ([Grow light](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Grow_light); [Hydroponics](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Hydroponics)).
- **Choose cold crops.** Potatoes, kale, leeks, turnips, swedes, Jerusalem artichokes, broad beans and oats tolerate cold and low light ([Potato](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Potato); [Kale](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Kale); [Rutabaga](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Rutabaga); [Jerusalem artichoke](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Jerusalem_artichoke)); a root cellar keeps them ([Root cellar](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Root_cellar)).
- **Vitamin D.** Without sun everyone needs it from food or tablets: oily fish, eggs, liver; 10 micrograms a day is the NHS figure for adults in winter ([Vitamin D (NHS)](kiwix:nhs_uk/www.nhs.uk/conditions/vitamins-and-minerals/vitamin-d/)); children without it get rickets ([Rickets (WikiMed)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_medicine_maxi/Rickets)).
- **Ration.** Calories per person per day, written down, with children and the sick first ([Famine playbook](playbook:famine); [Food storage](page:food-storage); [Food module](module:food)).
- **Dark and mood.** Months of dim light bring seasonal depression; bright lamps when there is power, routine, work outdoors in the middle of the day, and watching each other ([Seasonal affective disorder](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Seasonal_affective_disorder); [Mental health module](module:mental-health)).

## Long term

What is unique to this winter is the light. Cold alone can be met with fuel and walls; a dimmed sky shortens the growing season as well, and the models for a large nuclear exchange have killing frosts cutting growing seasons by 10 to 40 days a year for five years and surface temperatures depressed for more than 25 years ([Nuclear winter](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Nuclear_winter)). The Little Ice Age, centuries of cold summers, was survived in Britain with hardier crops, more livestock and a great deal of hunger ([Little Ice Age](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Little_Ice_Age)); the Toba eruption 74,000 years ago may have reduced humanity to thousands ([Youngest Toba eruption](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Youngest_Toba_eruption)). The scale decides everything, and nobody will know it in the first year.

So the long plan is built on four things. Crops chosen for cold and low light, roots and brassicas and oats rather than anything that needs a warm summer, and every square metre under cover that materials allow ([Growing food module](module:growing-food)). Growing indoors under lights wherever there is power, because a battery and an LED panel over a tray of sprouts is the one crop that does not depend on the sky ([Grow light](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Grow_light)). A herd cut each autumn to what the stored hay will feed, with the rest killed and preserved while they still carry flesh ([Livestock module](module:livestock); [Butchery](page:butchery)). And fuel and food planned in three-year units: wood cut and drying, seed saved from what actually grew, a community that pools tunnels, tools and labour ([Community module](module:community)). The [long rebuild playbook](playbook:long-rebuild) is the rest, and the year-by-year shape of it is below.

## UK specifics

- **Britain's advantage** is the sea: the Gulf Stream and the ocean around us damp temperature swings, and the coast gives seaweed, shellfish and fish when fields fail ([Climate of the United Kingdom](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Climate_of_the_United_Kingdom); [Edible seaweed](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Edible_seaweed)).
- **Britain's disadvantage** is the north: a short growing season shortened further puts Scotland and the uplands first into famine, as in the 1690s ([Little Ice Age](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Little_Ice_Age)).
- **The house, the alerts and the payments.** Insulation standards, stove and flue rules, lawful oil and diesel storage, Cold-Health Alerts, Cold Weather Payments and warm hubs are all in the [severe winter playbook](playbook:severe-winter), and exist for as long as the state does.
- **Gas and oil** run out or stop being delivered before wood does; the register's gas scenario runs October to May ([NRR 2025, p. 62](doc:nrr-2025#page=62)).
- **The map** shows hospitals, water works and fuel stations for as long as they run ([overlay](map:?overlay=health&overlay=water&overlay=fuel)).

## Checklist

- [ ] This year's harvest brought in and stored for two years; seed set aside before anyone eats {#store-and-seed now}
- [ ] Stove, flue and carbon monoxide alarm checked sound; a season's fuel by the door {#stove-ready now}
- [ ] Wood cut and stacked to dry a year ahead, because green wood will not burn; coppice planted {#wood-a-year-ahead now}
- [ ] Cold crops chosen and sown: potatoes, kale, leeks, roots, broad beans, oats {#cold-crops today}
- [ ] Greenhouse, tunnel or cold frames built; grow lights and trays ready for when there is power {#grow-under-cover today}
- [ ] Vitamin D for every child and adult: fish, eggs, liver, tablets while they last {#vitamin-d today}
- [ ] Daily routine, midday light and a watch on everyone's mood {#mood-and-routine today}
- [ ] Loft, walls, floors and windows insulated as far as materials allow {#insulate-the-house week}
- [ ] Herd cut to what stored hay will feed; the rest killed and preserved in autumn {#efficient-animals week}
- [ ] Pipes buried or lagged; indoor water store; snow-melting routine {#water-in-cold week}
- [ ] Wind or hydro power explored; solar expectations lowered {#power-without-sun week}
