## Right now

**Know what it is.** Soot from burning cities after a nuclear exchange, sulphur from a very large eruption, or dust from an asteroid strike, lofted high enough to stay for years, cools the surface by several degrees and cuts the light that crops need ([Nuclear winter](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Nuclear_winter); [Impact winter](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Impact_winter)). The signs are a sun that stays dim and red for weeks, frosts in the wrong months and a failed harvest. Tambora's 1815 eruption gave 1816 frosts in July and famine across Europe ([Year Without a Summer](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Year_Without_a_Summer)); the dimming of 536 brought eighteen months without proper sun, then famine and plague ([Volcanic winter of 536](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Volcanic_winter_of_536)).

**The first decisions are about this year's food and next year's seed.** Harvest everything that can be harvested, store it as if it must last two years, and set aside seed before anyone eats it ([Growing food module](module:growing-food)). Whatever caused it, the immediate playbook is the one for the cause: [nuclear war](playbook:nuclear-war), [volcanic ash](playbook:volcanic) or [severe winter](playbook:severe-winter).

**Accept the argument and plan for the worse case.** The 1987 survival manual called nuclear winter a myth ([NWSS, p. 23](doc:nwss#page=23)); the climate models say a large exchange cools the world for years ([Nuclear winter](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Nuclear_winter)). A household that prepared for the cold and got a mild year lost nothing.

## First 72 hours

- **Fuel.** A winter that lasts years is heated with wood, and wood is cut a year ahead to dry; start coppice and stacks now ([Coppicing](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Coppicing); [Wood fuel](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Wood_fuel)). Coal and peat where they exist ([Peat](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Peat)). A stove with a safe flue and a carbon monoxide alarm ([Approved Document J, p. 43](doc:ad-j#page=43)).
- **Insulation.** One warm room now; then the whole house to the current standard as materials allow: loft, walls, floors, windows ([Approved Document L volume 1](doc:ad-l1); [Building insulation](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Building_insulation)).
- **Water** freezes: bury pipes, keep a trickle, store indoors, and melt snow ([Water module](module:water)).
- **Power.** Solar panels give a fraction of their output under a dimmed sky; wind and micro-hydro do not care about the light ([Micro hydro](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Micro_hydro); [Wind turbine](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Wind_turbine); [Power module](module:power)).
- **Animals** eat what people cannot: hay, tops, scraps; rabbits and hens are the efficient ones; goats give milk on rough grazing ([Livestock module](module:livestock)).
- **The register's** nearest scenarios are a nuclear war elsewhere with global economic and migration effects ([NRR 2025, p. 185](doc:nrr-2025#page=185)) and a week of deep snow ([NRR 2025, p. 143](doc:nrr-2025#page=143)); a years-long winter is beyond it.

## First month

- **Grow under cover.** Greenhouses, polytunnels, cold frames and cloches gain weeks of season and degrees of warmth ([Greenhouse](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Greenhouse); [Cold frame](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Cold_frame)); indoors, sprouted seeds and microgreens grow in days with little light ([Sprouting](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Sprouting); [Microgreen](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Microgreen)); mushrooms need no light at all ([Fungiculture](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Fungiculture)).
- **Choose cold crops.** Potatoes, kale, leeks, turnips, swedes, Jerusalem artichokes, broad beans and oats tolerate cold and low light ([Potato](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Potato); [Kale](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Kale); [Rutabaga](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Rutabaga); [Jerusalem artichoke](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Jerusalem_artichoke)); a root cellar keeps them ([Root cellar](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Root_cellar)).
- **Vitamin D.** Without sun everyone needs it from food or tablets: oily fish, eggs, liver; 10 micrograms a day is the NHS figure for adults in winter ([Vitamin D (NHS)](kiwix:nhs_uk/www.nhs.uk/conditions/vitamins-and-minerals/vitamin-d/)); children without it get rickets ([Rickets (NHS)](kiwix:nhs_uk/www.nhs.uk/conditions/rickets-and-osteomalacia/)).
- **Ration.** Calories per person per day, written down, with children and the sick first ([Famine playbook](playbook:famine); [Food module](module:food)).
- **Dark and mood.** Months of dim light bring seasonal depression; bright lamps when there is power, routine, work outdoors in the middle of the day, and watching each other ([Seasonal affective disorder](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Seasonal_affective_disorder); [Mental health module](module:mental-health)).

## Long term

The Little Ice Age, centuries of cold summers, was survived in Britain with hardier crops, more livestock and a great deal of hunger ([Little Ice Age](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Little_Ice_Age)); the Toba eruption 74,000 years ago may have reduced humanity to thousands ([Youngest Toba eruption](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Youngest_Toba_eruption)); the Chicxulub impact ended the dinosaurs ([Chicxulub crater](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Chicxulub_crater)). The scale decides everything, and nobody will know it in the first year. Plan in three-year units: fuel cut and drying, seed saved from what grew, a herd small enough to feed, a community that pools tunnels, tools and labour ([Community module](module:community)). The [long rebuild playbook](playbook:long-rebuild) is the rest.

## UK specifics

- **Britain's advantage** is the sea: the Gulf Stream and the ocean around us damp temperature swings, and the coast gives seaweed, shellfish and fish when fields fail ([Climate of the United Kingdom](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Climate_of_the_United_Kingdom); [Edible seaweed](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Edible_seaweed)).
- **Britain's disadvantage** is the north: a short growing season shortened further puts Scotland and the uplands first into famine, as in the 1690s ([Little Ice Age](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Little_Ice_Age)).
- **Housing stock** is old and leaky; the insulation standard for new homes shows what an existing one should aim at ([Approved Document L volume 1](doc:ad-l1)); stoves and flues have rules ([Approved Document J, p. 11](doc:ad-j#page=11)).
- **Gas and oil** run out or stop being delivered before wood does; the register's gas scenario runs October to May ([NRR 2025, p. 62](doc:nrr-2025#page=62)).
- **Cold-Health Alerts** and Cold Weather Payments exist while the state does ([AWHP, p. 34](doc:awhp-2026#page=34); [Cold Weather Payment](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/cold-weather-payment)); the hospitals, water works and fuel stations are on the map for as long as they run ([overlay](map:?overlay=health&overlay=water&overlay=fuel)).

## Checklist

- [ ] This year's harvest stored for two years; seed set aside before anyone eats {#store-and-seed}
- [ ] Wood cut and stacked to dry a year ahead; coppice planted {#wood-a-year-ahead}
- [ ] Stove, flue and carbon monoxide alarm sound; a season's fuel by the door {#stove-ready}
- [ ] Loft, walls, floors and windows insulated as far as materials allow {#insulate-the-house}
- [ ] Greenhouse, tunnel or cold frames built; sprouting jars and mushroom beds indoors {#grow-under-cover}
- [ ] Cold crops chosen: potatoes, kale, leeks, roots, broad beans, oats {#cold-crops}
- [ ] Vitamin D for every child and adult: fish, eggs, liver, tablets while they last {#vitamin-d}
- [ ] Rabbits, hens and a goat on food people cannot eat; hay stored {#efficient-animals}
- [ ] Pipes buried or lagged; indoor water store; snow-melting routine {#water-in-cold}
- [ ] Wind or hydro power explored; solar expectations lowered {#power-without-sun}
- [ ] Daily routine, midday light and a watch on everyone's mood {#mood-and-routine}
