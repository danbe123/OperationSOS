## Right now

**Count the calories, not the tins.** An adult needs about 2,000 kcal a day for women and 2,500 for men ([The Eatwell Guide (NHS)](kiwix:nhs_uk/www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/)); add up what the store holds in days at that rate and ration from today, because a store spent in the first month is gone before the first harvest ([Food module](module:food)). Children, pregnant women and the sick are fed first.

**Plant now, whatever the month.** Potatoes give 2 to 3 kg a square metre; anything sown today is food in three months; the hungry gap is April and May ([Growing food module](module:growing-food)). The 1940 Dig for Victory campaign turned lawns and parks into 1.4 million allotments ([Victory garden](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Victory_garden)).

**Do not eat what you cannot name.** Hemlock water dropwort and death cap kill every year; no fungus on the strength of a photograph, and nothing from roadsides, dog areas or sprayed fields ([FSA safe foraging](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/government/publications/guidance-for-safe-foraging/guidance-for-safe-foraging)).

## First 72 hours

- **Why it happens here.** The register's food and farming risks are contamination of the food chain ([NRR 2025, p. 122](doc:nrr-2025#page=122)), foot and mouth, with 1.9 million animals culled on 2,900 premises in the planning case and six million in 2001 ([NRR 2025, p. 160](doc:nrr-2025#page=160)), avian influenza with eight million birds lost over six to eight months ([NRR 2025, p. 162](doc:nrr-2025#page=162)), African swine fever ([NRR 2025, p. 166](doc:nrr-2025#page=166)), plant pests ([NRR 2025, p. 168](doc:nrr-2025#page=168)) and three-dry-winter drought ([NRR 2025, p. 151](doc:nrr-2025#page=151); [heat and drought playbook](playbook:heat-drought)). Nitrogen fertiliser is made from natural gas by the Haber process, so a gas crisis is a harvest crisis a year later ([Haber process](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Haber_process); [NRR 2025, p. 62](doc:nrr-2025#page=62)).
- **Ration.** The wartime ration was small and the population was healthier for it: registration with one shop, points for tins, and fair shares ([Rationing in the United Kingdom](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Rationing_in_the_United_Kingdom)). Run the household the same way: a weekly allowance per person, written down.
- **Preserve every glut.** Drying, salting, pickling, jam; low-acid vegetables and meat only in a pressure canner or they can grow botulism ([USDA canning](kiwix:usda-2015_en/home); [Botulism](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Botulism)).
- **Water** for people, animals and crops in that order; a drought-year garden is watered at the roots in the evening ([Water module](module:water)).
- **Keep animals lawful and alive.** Register any birds you keep, obey housing orders during bird flu, and know the foot-and-mouth rules on movement ([Register as a bird keeper](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/government/collections/register-as-a-bird-keeper); [Bird flu](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/guidance/bird-flu-avian-influenza-latest-situation-in-england)).

## First month

- **Foraging within the law.** Picking wild fruit, foliage, fungi and flowers for your own use is not theft ([Theft Act 1968 s4](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/section/4)); uprooting without permission is an offence and protected plants may not be picked ([Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 s13](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/69/section/13)). Nettles, blackberries, rosehips (the wartime vitamin C), hazelnuts, sweet chestnuts, acorns leached of their tannin, seaweed and shellfish from clean water are the calories and vitamins that grow wild in Britain ([Urtica dioica](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Urtica_dioica); [Rose hip](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Rose_hip); [Acorn](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Acorn); [Edible seaweed](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Edible_seaweed)); shellfish carry poisoning and norovirus ([Paralytic shellfish poisoning](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Paralytic_shellfish_poisoning)). The rules are on [Foraging law](page:foraging-law).
- **Fishing and shooting.** A rod licence and the owner's permission in England and Wales, the close season on rivers, and the Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975 ([Rod fishing rules](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/freshwater-rod-fishing-rules); [Salmon and Freshwater Fisheries Act 1975](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1975/51/contents)); rabbits and pigeons with the landowner's permission, deer only in season with a certificated rifle, no dogs for hunting mammals ([GOV.UK hunting](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/hunting); [Hunting Act 2004](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2004/37/contents); [Deer Act 1991](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/54/contents)). Rabbits, wood pigeons and grey squirrels are the abundant wild meat ([European rabbit](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/European_rabbit); [Common wood pigeon](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Common_wood_pigeon)).
- **Nutrition.** Signs of malnutrition are weight loss, tiredness, poor healing and infections; children stop growing ([Malnutrition (NHS)](kiwix:nhs_uk/www.nhs.uk/conditions/malnutrition/)). Scurvy from no fresh food in three months (bleeding gums, bruising) is cured by any vitamin C ([Scurvy (NHS)](kiwix:nhs_uk/www.nhs.uk/conditions/scurvy/)); rickets in children from no vitamin D is prevented by sun and oily fish ([Rickets (NHS)](kiwix:nhs_uk/www.nhs.uk/conditions/rickets-and-osteomalacia/)). Fat and protein run out before starch: eggs, milk, pulses, nuts and fish are the priority ([Medical module](module:medical)). Skipping meals under strict rationing brings on low blood sugar — shaking, sweating and confusion — treated with fast sugar then a proper meal ([Low blood sugar card](card:low-blood-sugar)).
- **Community kitchens.** The wartime British Restaurants fed a hot meal for a set price to anyone; a village hall with one cooker does the same ([British Restaurant](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/British_Restaurant); [Community module](module:community)).

## Long term

Ireland lost a million people to hunger and a million to emigration between 1845 and 1852 when blight took the one crop most people ate ([Great Famine (Ireland)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Great_Famine_(Ireland)); [Phytophthora infestans](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Phytophthora_infestans)); the Dutch Hunger Winter of 1944 to 1945 killed 18,000 in a modern country cut off from food ([Dutch famine of 1944–1945](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Dutch_famine_of_1944–1945)). The lessons are the same: many crops not one, seed saved from open-pollinated varieties, soil fed with compost, manure and nitrogen-fixing beans instead of bought fertiliser, rotation to break disease, and animals that turn what people cannot eat into eggs, milk and meat ([Growing food module](module:growing-food); [Crop rotation](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Crop_rotation); [Nitrogen fixation](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Nitrogen_fixation)). Feeding a starving person is itself dangerous: small frequent meals for the first days, or refeeding syndrome can kill ([Refeeding syndrome](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Refeeding_syndrome)). The [long rebuild playbook](playbook:long-rebuild) is the decade after.

## UK specifics

- **The law of the land.** Foraging for your own use is lawful; selling it is not; uprooting needs permission; Schedule 8 plants are protected ([Theft Act 1968 s4](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/section/4); [Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 s13](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1981/69/section/13)). Open access land in England and Wales and Scotland's right of responsible access let you walk, not pick crops or take game ([CRoW Act 2000 s2](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/37/section/2); [access land overlay](map:?overlay=access-land&overlay=footpaths)). Taking crops from a field is theft ([Theft Act 1968 s1](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1968/60/section/1)).
- **Animals.** The Animal Welfare Act duty of care applies in a famine ([Animal Welfare Act 2006](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/45/contents)); killing your own animal for your own table is lawful with proper stunning ([WATOK 2015](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1782/contents)); pigs need a holding number ([Keeping a pet pig](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/guidance/keeping-a-pet-pig-or-micropig)).
- **Disease.** Foot and mouth closed the countryside in 2001 ([2001 United Kingdom foot-and-mouth outbreak](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/2001_United_Kingdom_foot-and-mouth_outbreak)); housing orders during bird flu are law ([Bird flu](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/guidance/bird-flu-avian-influenza-latest-situation-in-england)).
- **Precedent.** Britain rationed from 1940 to 1954 and imported less than it does now; the Ministry of Food, points and the Woolton pie are the working model ([Rationing in the United Kingdom](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Rationing_in_the_United_Kingdom); [Minister of Food](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Minister_of_Food)).
- **Water** for irrigation from rivers needs an abstraction licence above 20 cubic metres a day in England ([Water Industry Act 1991](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1991/56/contents)); rainwater and grey water are yours ([Water module](module:water)).

## Checklist

- [ ] Store counted in days at 2,000 to 2,500 kcal a person; weekly ration written {#count-calories}
- [ ] Every bed, tub and lawn edge planted; seed potatoes chitting; seed saved {#plant-everything}
- [ ] Compost heap, manure source and nitrogen-fixing beans in the rotation {#feed-the-soil}
- [ ] Hens registered; housing-order rules known; rabbits or goats considered {#animals-lawful}
- [ ] Pressure canner or drying racks ready for the glut; botulism rule learned {#preserve-safely}
- [ ] Foraging rules and the two deadly plants learned by every adult {#foraging-rules}
- [ ] Rod licence, permission and seasons checked before fishing or shooting {#fishing-and-shooting-law}
- [ ] Vitamin C and vitamin D sources for children planned: rosehips, eggs, sun {#vitamins-for-children}
- [ ] Community kitchen or shared cooker organised in the hall {#community-kitchen}
- [ ] Refeeding rule known: small meals first for anyone who has gone hungry {#refeeding-rule}
