## The first month

**Do not start here.** The first month belongs to the playbook for whatever caused this — the [grid collapse](playbook:grid-collapse), the [pandemic](playbook:pandemic), the [famine](playbook:famine) or another — and to the ordinary order of work: shelter and water together, then sanitation, then food, then security ([Water module](module:water); [Sanitation module](module:sanitation)). Recovery, in the National Risk Register's own words, "can last months, years or even decades", and works best where the affected community takes part in deciding how ([NRR 2025, p. 22](doc:nrr-2025#page=22)).

Two things belong to the rebuild even in month one: **write everything down** from the first week, because the disputes of year three are settled by the ledger started now ([Community module](module:community)); and **keep this box alive**, because it is the library the rest of this depends on ([Keeping the box alive](page:rebuild-keeping-the-box)).

## The first year

Water, food, fuel and warmth at village rather than household scale, a register of who can do what, a rota, a charter, and a way of burying the dead — all of it is on [The first year](page:rebuild-first-year). The lever that saves most lives is unglamorous: keeping human waste out of the water. Liverpool's life expectancy was 19 years before its sewers and had more than doubled within a working lifetime after them ([History of water supply and sanitation](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/History_of_water_supply_and_sanitation); [Medicine without industry](page:rebuild-medicine)).

Print while there is still power and ink: fire, water, sanitation, first aid, seed saving, soap, lime, charcoal, units and the calendar, one sheet each ([The essentials, printed](page:rebuild-essentials-printed)).

## Years one to three: food and health

The rebuild stands or falls on calories. Get the arithmetic, the rotation, the fertility without bought inputs and the seed bank right, and plan for a quarter to a third of modern yields once industrial nitrogen is gone, rising towards a half once the leys and the muck are working. Do the calorie sum on 3,000 kcal a day for anyone working the land, not on 2,000 ([Farming for a decade](page:rebuild-farming); [British Agricultural Revolution](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/British_Agricultural_Revolution); [Growing food module](module:growing-food); [Livestock module](module:livestock)). Never live off one crop, store a full year rather than a season, and set next year's seed aside before anyone eats this year's ([Food storage](page:food-storage)).

Health in the same years is midwifery, teeth, wounds and clean water, plus honest limits on what medicine a village can make ([Medicine without industry](page:rebuild-medicine); [Where There Is No Doctor](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor); [Medical module](module:medical)). Train two people to every skill, so no trade is one death from being lost.

## Years three to ten: trades and power

The materials ladder, in the order a village needs it: soap and lye, charcoal, lime and mortar, pottery and glass, alcohol, paper, textiles, salt and preserving ([Making things again](page:rebuild-making-things); [Appropedia](kiwix:appropedia_en_all/Welcome_to_Appropedia)). Metal is scrap before ore: a blacksmith working salvaged steel on a charcoal forge comes years before anyone smelts iron ([Iron and tools](page:rebuild-iron-and-tools)). Power comes back as a water wheel, a wind machine, salvaged panels and scrap motors before it comes back as a grid ([Power from scratch](page:rebuild-power); [Power module](module:power)). Coppice cut on rotation gives firewood for ever, and the wood is cut a year ahead to dry ([Coppicing](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Coppicing)).

Alongside the trades, the institutions: rules people recognise, a way of settling disputes, land held on terms everyone knows, records, and barter or commodity money for trade with the next valley ([Law, records and trade](page:rebuild-law-and-trade); [Security and the law module](module:security-law)).

## The decade after

This is where knowledge is either passed on or lost. Collapses are usually slow and partial, and what carries people through is the parish, the ledger and the school ([Societal collapse](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Societal_collapse)). Teach children from the library while it still runs, and print before it stops ([Keeping the box alive](page:rebuild-keeping-the-box); [The library map](page:rebuild-library-map)). Get the method back, not just the recipes: controlled comparisons, written records, and remade units and instruments so that measurements mean the same thing in two villages ([Restarting science](page:rebuild-restarting-science); [The Knowledge](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/The_Knowledge:_How_to_Rebuild_Our_World_from_Scratch)).

## Checklist

- [ ] The cause's own playbook worked through before this one is started {#cause-playbook hour}
- [ ] This box on a panel and a battery, and a second copy of the drive stored dry somewhere else {#box-alive today}
- [ ] Ledger opened: decisions, stores, who is owed what, who did what {#ledger-open today}
- [ ] Register of skills, tools, land, seed and animals, on paper in the hall {#skills-register week}
- [ ] Village water source found, filtered and disinfected; latrines dug well away from it and downhill {#water-and-latrines week}
- [ ] The one-page essentials printed while there is still power and ink {#essentials-printed week}
- [ ] Ground measured and the calorie arithmetic done for the people you actually have {#calorie-sum week}
- [ ] Three staples planted, never one: potatoes, a grain and beans {#three-staples week}
- [ ] Seed bank started, open-pollinated, two years held, split between two buildings {#seed-bank week}
- [ ] A year's harvest stored: grain dried and rodent-proof, roots clamped, seed set aside first {#year-stored week}
- [ ] Two people apprenticed to each of midwifery, wound care, teeth and the pharmacy {#two-per-skill week}
- [ ] Coppice cut on rotation and a year of firewood drying {#fuel-rotation week}
- [ ] Water wheel, wind machine or salvaged panels keeping the lights and the library on {#village-power week}
- [ ] School running from the library, and the reading order on the library map followed {#school-running week}
