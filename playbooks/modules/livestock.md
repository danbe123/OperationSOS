## Key facts

- Every bird keeper in Great Britain must register, even with a single hen ([register as a bird keeper](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/government/collections/register-as-a-bird-keeper)); housing orders can be imposed during avian influenza outbreaks ([bird flu latest situation](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/guidance/bird-flu-avian-influenza-latest-situation-in-england)).
- Foot-and-mouth disease in 2001 closed the countryside for months and led to six million animals being culled ([2001 United Kingdom foot-and-mouth outbreak](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/2001_United_Kingdom_foot-and-mouth_outbreak)); bluetongue and avian influenza are the current equivalent risks ([NRR 2025, p. 160](doc:nrr-2025#page=160)).
- The Animal Welfare Act 2006 duty of care applies to any animal you keep, whatever else is happening ([Animal Welfare Act 2006](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/45/contents)).

## What to do

1. Four to six hens will give three to five eggs a day in summer and far fewer in winter; each needs about 0.3 litres of water a day.
2. Goats provide milk but need 5 to 10 litres of water a day each; rabbits are the fastest source of home-reared protein.
3. If you plan to keep pigs, you need a county parish holding number and movement licences before you start ([keeping a pet pig](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/guidance/keeping-a-pet-pig-or-micropig)).
4. Keep bees for honey and wax if you have the space and are willing to learn the basics first ([beekeeping](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Beekeeping)).
5. Killing an animal for your own consumption at home is lawful only with proper stunning, under the 2015 regulations ([Welfare of Animals at the Time of Killing Regulations 2015](kiwix:legislation_uk/www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1782/contents)).

## UK specifics

- Fencing, protection from foxes, and enough fodder and hay to see animals through winter all need planning well before the cold arrives.
- Use the pets Q&A for animal first aid questions as they come up ([pets Q&A](kiwix:pets.stackexchange.com_en_all/questions)).
- Registration, housing orders and movement licences above apply across Great Britain, and outbreaks of disease can close markets and movement with very little notice ([bird flu latest situation](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/guidance/bird-flu-avian-influenza-latest-situation-in-england)).
