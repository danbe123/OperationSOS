## Key facts

- Sow what the month allows: nothing tender (runner and French beans, courgettes, squash, sweetcorn, tomatoes, chillies) goes outdoors before mid-May, and only after a week of hardening off, because one late frost kills the lot; hardy crops and sowing under cover fill the other months ([hardening off](kiwix:gardening.stackexchange.com_en_all/questions/23182/what-is-hardening-off); [chilli seeds in the UK](kiwix:gardening.stackexchange.com_en_all/questions/2261/when-to-plant-chili-seeds-in-the-uk); [Tomato](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Tomato)).
- April and May are the "hungry gap", when stored food from last year has run out and this year's crops are not yet ready ([hungry gap](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Hungry_gap)).
- The National Risk Register lists disruption and contamination of the food supply among its reasonable worst-case risks, which is part of why growing even a little matters ([NRR 2025, p. 122](doc:nrr-2025#page=122)).

## What to do

1. Chit seed potatoes from February; plant first earlies late March to April and lift them in June and July, and plant maincrop in April for a September harvest. Sow broad beans in November or February, peas from March to June, onion sets in March, garlic in October or November, carrots April to July, and kale and leeks in April or May for a winter crop.
2. Rotate crops over a three- or four-bed system to break the build-up of disease in the soil ([crop rotation](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Crop_rotation)).
3. Build fertility without bought fertiliser: compost, manure, urine diluted one part to ten of water, wood ash, comfrey, and nitrogen-fixing beans and green manures.
4. Save your own open-pollinated seed rather than F1 hybrids, which will not breed true the following year ([seed saving](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Seed_saving)).
5. Extend the season with cloches, cold frames or a polytunnel, and ask specific questions of the gardening Q&A as they come up ([gardening Q&A](kiwix:gardening.stackexchange.com_en_all/questions)).

## UK specifics

- A traditional 10-rod allotment, about 250 square metres, was sized to cover a family's vegetable needs for the year, and councils have a duty to provide allotments to meet demand ([allotment](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Allotment_(gardening)); [Food storage](page:food-storage)). Potatoes give the most food energy per area of any staple, and farm yields average about 17 tonnes a hectare worldwide, roughly 1.7 kg per square metre ([Potato](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Potato)).
- Slugs, pigeons and rabbits are the main UK pests to plan around, more than drought or heat in most years.
- Indoors, sprouted seeds and mushrooms extend what you can grow without any ground at all ([fungiculture](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Fungiculture)), and on the coast edible seaweed is a further free source of food ([edible seaweed](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Edible_seaweed)).
