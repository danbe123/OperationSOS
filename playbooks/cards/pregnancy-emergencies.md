## When to use

Vaginal bleeding in pregnancy, severe headache or swelling late on, a fit, or heavy bleeding after birth.

## Steps

1. Lie her down on her left side. Note how much blood and any pain.
2. Heavy bleeding, severe pain, faint or collapsed — [[call 999]].
3. Light bleeding: rest, pads not tampons, and [[call 111]] or the midwife.
4. Early pregnancy: bleeding with cramps is usually a miscarriage; a lot of pain with little blood, one-sided, with faintness or shoulder-tip pain, is an ectopic pregnancy that can bleed inside and kill — [[call 999]] ([Ship Captain's Medical Guide ch. 9, p. 2](doc:scmg-ch09#page=2); [Ectopic pregnancy (WikiMed)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_medicine_maxi/Ectopic_pregnancy)).
5. Bleeding in the second half of pregnancy means the placenta may be bleeding: left side, nothing in the vagina, no internal examination — [[call 999]] ([Where There Is No Doctor, p. 298](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=298); [Ship Captain's Medical Guide ch. 9, p. 2](doc:scmg-ch09#page=2)).
6. After 20 weeks: a severe headache, flashing lights or blurred vision, swelling of the face and hands, pain under the ribs or vomiting can be pre-eclampsia, dangerous for mother and baby — [[call 999]] if severe, otherwise the midwife or [[call 111]] the same day ([Pre-eclampsia (WikiMed)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_medicine_maxi/Pre-eclampsia); [Where There Is No Doctor, p. 298](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=298)).
7. A fit (eclampsia): protect her from injury, left side afterwards, [seizures](card:seizures) — [[call 999]]; fits usually last about a minute but can recur ([Eclampsia (WikiMed)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_medicine_maxi/Eclampsia)).
8. Heavy bleeding after birth, more than 500 ml or soaking pads fast ([Postpartum bleeding (WikiMed)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_medicine_maxi/Postpartum_bleeding)): rub the womb firmly through the belly below the navel until it hardens, put the baby to the breast, help her empty her bladder, lie her flat and treat for [shock](card:shock) — [[call 999]] ([Where There Is No Doctor, p. 314](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=314); [childbirth](card:childbirth)).

## Warnings

**Warning:** Never put anything into the vagina or examine internally when there is bleeding late in pregnancy ([Where There Is No Doctor, p. 298](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=298)).
**Warning:** Never pull on the cord to deliver the placenta ([Where There Is No Doctor, p. 313](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=313)).

## Stop or escalate

Heavy bleeding at any stage, severe abdominal pain, collapse, a fit, or pre-eclampsia symptoms — [[call 999]]. Light bleeding or worry — the midwife or [[call 111]]. If nobody can come: miscarriage: rest, painkillers, watch for days for heavy bleeding with clots or fever, which means tissue is left inside and needs antibiotics ([Where There Is No Doctor, p. 330](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=330); [Ship Captain's Medical Guide ch. 9, p. 2](doc:scmg-ch09#page=2)); bleeding after birth: keep massaging until the womb stays hard, breastfeed, and if it continues press down with both hands and your weight just below the navel, then squeeze the womb forward against the pubic bone; oxytocin or ergometrine if you hold them and the placenta is out ([Where There Is No Doctor, p. 314](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=314); [Survival and Austere Medicine, p. 288](doc:survival-austere-medicine-2017#page=288)); pre-eclampsia: bed rest, quiet and dark, and only delivery cures it ([Where There Is No Doctor, p. 298](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=298); [Eclampsia (WikiMed)](kiwix:wikipedia_en_medicine_maxi/Eclampsia)).
