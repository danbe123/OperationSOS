## When to use

Pale clammy skin, fast weak pulse, fast breathing, dizziness, thirst or confusion after injury or bleeding.

## Steps

1. Send for help — [[call 999]]. Stop bleeding, cool burns.
2. Lie them down on a blanket. Raise the legs about 30 cm.
3. Keep the legs flat if a leg, pelvis or head is injured.
4. Loosen tight clothing at the neck, chest and waist.
5. Keep them warm with blankets, but do not overheat them.
6. Nothing to eat or drink; moisten the lips if they are thirsty.
7. Reassure, stay with them, and check breathing and pulse every few minutes; if they become unresponsive but breathe, use the [recovery position](card:recovery-position).

## Warnings

**Warning:** Shock can develop slowly; a person who seems fine after a big bleed may collapse an hour later.
**Warning:** This is not emotional shock; it is failing circulation and it kills.

## Stop or escalate

Shock kills without treatment, so get help at once — [[call 999]]. If breathing stops, start [CPR](card:cpr-adult). If nobody can come: keep them lying with the feet a little higher than the head (half-sitting instead if the head is injured), warm, every bleed stopped and every wound dressed; once they are awake and can swallow, give sips of water or rehydration drink, and lots of it if they are dehydrated or burned ([dehydration card](card:dehydration)); give paracetamol rather than anything sedating for pain, and if you know how, fluid into a vein at a fast drip ([WTIND, p. 126](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=126)). Sepsis causes the same picture with fever ([sepsis card](card:sepsis)).
