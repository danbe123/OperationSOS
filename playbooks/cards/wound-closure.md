## When to use

A cut whose edges gape, once the bleeding is controlled and it is clean; deciding whether and how to close it.

## Steps

1. Stop the bleeding first with firm pressure on a clean pad.
2. Clean it thoroughly ([wound cleaning](card:wound-cleaning)).
3. Close only if it is clean, fresh, and the edges meet without pulling.
4. Do not close: bites, punctures, crush wounds, dirty wounds, wounds older than 6 hours, anything with grit or glass left inside, or a wound already red or oozing; dress these open and let them heal from the bottom ([Ship Captain's Medical Guide ch. 4, p. 4](doc:scmg-ch04#page=4); [Where There Is No Doctor, p. 135](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=135)).
5. Strips (Steri-Strips, or plaster tape cut into narrow strips): dry the skin, stick one end beside the wound, pull the edges together, stick the other side; repeat along the cut with gaps between strips for fluid to escape ([Ship Captain's Medical Guide ch. 4, p. 3](doc:scmg-ch04#page=3)).
6. Cover with a dry dressing and keep it dry. Leave strips on for 5 days on the face, 10 on the body and 14 on a hand or foot, the same as stitches ([Where There Is No Doctor, p. 135](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=135)).
7. Check daily. Redness, heat, pus, a red line or fever: take the strips off, let it open and treat as infected ([Where There Is No Doctor, p. 135](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=135); [sepsis](card:sepsis)).
8. Stitches only when no help is coming, the wound is deep, clean and gapes despite strips, and you follow Where There Is No Doctor: boil a needle and nylon thread for 20 minutes, start in the middle, stitches at least 1 cm apart and 0.6 cm from the edge, never over a hollow beneath ([Where There Is No Doctor, p. 135](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=135); [Ship Captain's Medical Guide ch. 4, p. 4](doc:scmg-ch04#page=4)).

## Warnings

**Warning:** Deep cuts to the face, hands, over a joint, or with numbness or a finger that will not move need a professional: nerves and tendons may be cut.
**Warning:** When in doubt, do not close it. A wound left open heals; a dirty wound closed becomes an abscess ([Ship Captain's Medical Guide ch. 4, p. 4](doc:scmg-ch04#page=4)).

## Stop or escalate

Bleeding that will not stop with pressure, or a deep wound of the neck, chest or belly — [[call 999]] ([severe bleeding](card:severe-bleeding)). A gaping cut, glass in a wound, a cut on the face or hand, or numbness or weakness beyond it — [[call 111]] or the urgent treatment centre within hours, while it can still be closed. If nobody can come: pack a dirty or old wound loosely with damp clean gauze, re-dress it on alternate days, and close it by delayed primary closure 3 to 5 days later once it is clean and pink ([Emergency War Surgery, p. 124](doc:emergency-war-surgery-2018#page=124); [Ship Captain's Medical Guide ch. 4, p. 4](doc:scmg-ch04#page=4)); bites stay open ([bites and stings](card:bites-stings)).
