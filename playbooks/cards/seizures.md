## When to use

Someone is shaking or jerking uncontrollably, has gone stiff, or is unresponsive with twitching or staring.

## Steps

1. Note the time it started. Move hard objects away.
2. Cushion the head with something soft. Do not hold them down.
3. Do not put anything in their mouth. Loosen clothing at the neck.
4. Stay with them and let the seizure run its course.
5. When the jerking stops, put them in the [recovery position](card:recovery-position) and check breathing.
6. Stay until they are fully awake; they will be confused and tired. Tell them what happened.
7. If they have a known epilepsy plan or rescue medicine, follow it.

## Warnings

**Warning:** A seizure lasting more than 5 minutes, or one after another without waking, is a medical emergency.
**Warning:** Do not give food, drink or tablets until they are fully awake.

## Stop or escalate

999 if it lasts over 5 minutes, repeats, they are injured, it happened in water, it is their first seizure, they are pregnant or diabetic, or breathing does not return to normal.
