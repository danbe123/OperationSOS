## When to use

Skin is burned by heat, steam, hot liquid, chemicals or electricity.

## Steps

1. Move away from the heat. Stop, drop and roll if clothing is alight.
2. Cool the burn under cool running water for 20 minutes.
3. Take off jewellery and loose clothing near it; leave stuck cloth.
4. {{#if water}}Cooling helps up to three hours after the burn.{{else}}With the mains off, pour from bottles, the bath or a water butt: any clean cool water will do, and cooling still helps up to three hours after the burn.{{/if}} Never use ice, butter, creams or toothpaste.
5. Cover loosely with cling film laid lengthways, or a clean non-fluffy cloth or plastic bag. Do not wrap tightly.
6. Give paracetamol for pain: for an adult two 500 mg tablets, at least four hours apart and no more than four times in 24 hours ([NHS paracetamol](kiwix:nhs_medicines/www.nhs.uk/medicines/paracetamol-for-adults/)). Keep the person warm; a big burn loses heat fast.
7. Chemical burns: blot or brush the substance off dry first, then rinse only if the skin itches or burns, and for about an hour, not twenty minutes; keep the run-off away from you ([chemical exposure](card:chemical-exposure)).

{{#unless power}}
Cool water is all this needs, and it does not need to be heated, pumped or lit. Work by head torch and keep naked flames away from a burned person and from any oxygen. The twenty minutes of cooling chills the whole body, so warm the rest of them while the burn is cooling: blankets over everything except the burn, and the warmest room in the house afterwards. Keep clean water back for later dressing changes rather than using every drop now.
{{/unless}}

{{#if scenario:chemical}}
Get the chemical off before you treat the burn: clothing off and cut away rather than pulled over the head, dry powder brushed off, then rinse and keep rinsing, far longer than for a heat burn and for as long as the skin stings ([chemical exposure](card:chemical-exposure)). Keep the run-off away from you, from other people and from drinking water, bag the clothes and leave the bag outside. Do not put cling film on a burn that has not been rinsed clean.
{{/if}}

{{#if scenario:nuclear-war}}
Shelter comes first: a flash burn is survivable, standing in fallout to treat it is not. Get inside, take off the outer layer of clothing and bag it outside the room, which removes much of any contamination, wash exposed skin and hair gently with soap and water without scrubbing the burn, then treat the burn as on this card ([radiation sickness](card:radiation-sickness); [Radiation module](module:radiation)). Do not go outside for water while the fallout is coming down; use what is stored indoors.
{{/if}}

## Warnings

**Warning:** A burn larger than the person's hand, deep, white or charred, or on the face, hands, feet, genitals or across a joint needs hospital.
**Warning:** Do not burst blisters.

## Stop or escalate

Any large or deep burn, any electrical or chemical burn ([electric shock](card:electric-shock)), burns to the face or airway, difficulty breathing, or a burned baby, child or older person — [[call 999]]. If nobody can come: keep the burn clean under a loose clean cloth changed whenever it is soiled, honey spread on the burn and washed off and renewed twice a day helps prevent infection, and pus, a bad smell or fever is infection to treat with warm salt-water compresses and the antibiotics you hold ([WTIND, p. 145](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=145)); a burn bigger than twice the person's hand needs plenty to drink, ideally a salt-and-sugar drink, until they pass urine often, and protein-rich food ([WTIND, p. 146](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor#page=146); [dehydration card](card:dehydration)).

{{#unless phones}}
No ambulance and no burns unit. Cool for the twenty minutes, cover, keep them warm, and then decide by size: a burn larger than the person's hand, or any burn to the face, hands, feet, genitals or across a joint, needs carrying to the nearest crewed help on [the map's health layer](map:?overlay=health), because scarring across a joint and infection are what cost the limb. Take the salt-and-sugar drink with you and keep them drinking on the way. If nothing is reachable, the clean dressings, the fluids and the daily check for infection above are the treatment.
{{/unless}}
