## How we get warnings

## Warnings we do not trust: the SHARE checklist

Every emergency comes with rumours, doctored pictures and fake "official" messages. The National Risk Register asks everyone to run the government's SHARE checklist before believing or forwarding anything ([NRR 2025, p. 22](doc:nrr-2025#page=22)):

- **S**ource: is it from a source you trust, such as the emergency services, the council or the BBC?
- **H**eadline: read past the headline; does the rest say the same thing?
- **A**nalyse: check the facts against another source before you act on them.
- **R**etouched: could the picture or clip have been edited, or be from another time and place?
- **E**rror: spelling mistakes, odd formatting and strange addresses mark fakes.

When a scenario says "the SHARE checklist", this is what it means.
