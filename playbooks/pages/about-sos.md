## What it is

This is an offline, UK-focused knowledge box: playbooks for specific emergencies, a snapshot of the NHS website, Wikipedia, technical manuals and maps, all held on one small device. It works entirely on its own, over its own WiFi, with no internet connection needed at all. It draws its power from a mains adapter or, in a power cut, from the optional 20,000 mAh power bank, which runs it for roughly 8 to 15 hours depending on use ([What still works](page:what-still-works)).

## Where the content comes from

Every item in the library shows an "as at" date and a licence, so you can see how current it is and where it came from. The NHS pages are a dated snapshot published under the Open Government Licence v3, and each carries the line "Information from the NHS website ... as at" the date it was crawled. Map data comes from OpenStreetMap under the ODbL and from Ordnance Survey under the OGL. The playbooks themselves are written from the sources cited under each one, and every citation opens the source it was drawn from ([National Risk Register 2025](doc:nrr-2025); [Prepare](kiwix:prepare_uk/prepare.campaign.gov.uk/about-this-website-and-uk-resilience/)).

## What it is not

This box is not a medical device and does not give medical advice: it shows official pages and first-aid steps, and it tells you plainly when to call 999 or 111 instead. The built-in assistant, when switched on, only quotes from the library, and it can still be wrong; the library pages themselves are always the source of truth, not the assistant's summary of them. This box is not affiliated with the NHS or with any government department. It does no tracking, needs no account, and nothing on it leaves the box.

## Keeping it current

The owner runs an update over an ethernet cable when one is available, and the system screen shows when each item in the library was last refreshed. Emergency Alerts, weather warnings and flood warnings are live services provided by others and this box cannot replace them; what Emergency Alerts are and which phones receive them is on [What still works](page:what-still-works) ([Emergency Alerts](kiwix:govuk_resilience/www.gov.uk/alerts)).
