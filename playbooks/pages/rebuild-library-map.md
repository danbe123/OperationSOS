## How to use this page

This box holds more than anyone can read. For each domain below there are **three things to read, in the order given**: the first tells you what to do this week, the second is the working handbook, the third is the deep reference you go to with a specific question. Under each is a line of anchoring encyclopedia articles — the ones to print, copy or teach from if the library itself is ever at risk ([Keeping the box alive](page:rebuild-keeping-the-box); [The essentials, printed](page:rebuild-essentials-printed)).

Not everything here is on every copy of the box. The core library is always present; the extended shelf — LibreTexts, Khan Academy, Project Gutenberg and the Survivor Library of old trade manuals — is only on the larger builds, so check before you plan a trade around it ([About Operation SOS](page:about-sos)).

## Water

1. [Water module](module:water) — what a household and a street do first.
2. [Water disinfection](page:water-disinfection) — the one table of methods, doses and waiting times.
3. [Water Treatment Library](kiwix:zimgit-water_en/home) — filtration, storage and treatment at village scale.

Anchors: [Water purification](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Water_purification), [Slow sand filter](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Slow_sand_filter), [Well](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Well), [Hand pump](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Hand_pump).

## Sanitation

1. [Sanitation module](module:sanitation) — latrines, distances from water, hand washing.
2. [FM 4-25-12, Field Sanitation](doc:fm-4-25-12-field-sanitation) — how an army keeps a camp from killing itself; a village is the same problem.
3. [Approved Document H](doc:ad-h) — drains, septic tanks, cesspools and drainage fields, with the UK distances.

Anchors: [Sanitation](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Sanitation), [Composting toilet](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Composting_toilet), [Sewage treatment](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Sewage_treatment).

## Medicine

1. [Medicine without industry](page:rebuild-medicine) — what actually saves lives when the pharmacies are empty.
2. [Where There Is No Doctor](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor) — the village handbook: diagnosis, drugs, childbirth, children.
3. [Survival and Austere Medicine](doc:survival-austere-medicine-2017) — the harder end: anaesthesia, surgery, making and keeping drugs. Teeth have their own book, [Where There Is No Dentist](doc:where-there-is-no-dentist).

Anchors: [Sanitation](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Sanitation), [Germ theory of disease](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Germ_theory_of_disease), [Penicillin](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Penicillin), [Midwifery](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Midwifery). The medical encyclopedias are [WikiMed](kiwix:wikipedia_en_medicine_maxi/Sepsis) and [WikEM](kiwix:wikem_en_all_maxi/Main_Page).

## Surgery and first aid

1. [Medical module](module:medical) and the quick cards — [severe bleeding](card:severe-bleeding), [wound cleaning](card:wound-cleaning), [wound closure](card:wound-closure), [sepsis](card:sepsis).
2. [FM 4-25-11, First Aid](doc:fm-4-25-11-first-aid) — the drills, illustrated, for people with no training.
3. [Emergency War Surgery](doc:emergency-war-surgery-2018) — triage and damage control when there is no hospital; the [Special Forces Medical Handbook](doc:st-31-91b-sf-medical-handbook) covers the middle ground.

Anchors: [Antiseptic](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Antiseptic), [Debridement](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Debridement), [Triage](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Triage), [Tourniquet](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Tourniquet).

## Farming and food

1. [Farming for a decade](page:rebuild-farming) — the calorie arithmetic, rotation, fertility, seed.
2. [Growing food module](module:growing-food) and [Livestock module](module:livestock) — the sowing calendar and the animals, with UK law.
3. [Gardening Q&A](kiwix:gardening.stackexchange.com_en_all/questions) and the [CD3WD library](kiwix:cd3wdproject.org_en_all/www.cd3wdproject.org/) — specific answers, and the small-farm manuals: [grain storage](kiwix:cd3wdproject.org_en_all/www.cd3wdproject.org/VITAHTML/SUBLEV/EN1/GRNSTOR3.HTM) is the one to read before your first harvest.

Anchors: [Crop rotation](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Crop_rotation), [Potato](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Potato), [Seed saving](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Seed_saving), [Hungry gap](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Hungry_gap). Keeping the harvest is on [Food storage](page:food-storage) and [Butchery](page:butchery).

## Building

1. [Approved Document A](doc:ad-a) — what a wall, beam and roof will actually carry. Structure first, always.
2. [Approved Document B volume 1](doc:ad-b1) for escape and fire spread, and [Approved Document J](doc:ad-j) for flues, air supply and carbon monoxide — the two documents that stop a rebuilt house killing the family in it.
3. [Home Improvement Q&A](kiwix:diy.stackexchange.com_en_all/questions) and [Wikibooks Do-It-Yourself](kiwix:wikibooks_en_all_maxi/Do-It-Yourself) for the work itself.

Anchors: [Timber framing](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Timber_framing), [Cob](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Cob_(material)), [Wattle and daub](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Wattle_and_daub), [Lime mortar](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Lime_mortar), [Thatching](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Thatching).

## Maths and physics

1. [OpenStax Prealgebra](doc:openstax-prealgebra-2e) — arithmetic, fractions, percentages and measurement, which is what a village actually uses daily.
2. [OpenStax College Physics](doc:openstax-college-physics-2e) — forces, heat, electricity and optics, enough to design a wheel, a lever or a still.
3. [Wikibooks Mathematics](kiwix:wikibooks_en_all_maxi/Subject:Mathematics) and [Physics](kiwix:wikibooks_en_all_maxi/Subject:Physics) for teaching material, with [OpenStax Introductory Statistics](doc:openstax-introductory-statistics-2e) when you start comparing treatments and yields.

Anchors: [Arithmetic](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Arithmetic), [Pendulum](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Pendulum), [Metre](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Metre), [Kilogram](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Kilogram), [Scientific method](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Scientific_method). Remaking the units and instruments themselves is on [Restarting science](page:rebuild-restarting-science).

## Teaching children

1. [Keeping the box alive](page:rebuild-keeping-the-box) — teach from the library while it still runs, and print before it does not.

3. [Simple English Wikipedia](kiwix:wikipedia_en-simple_all_maxi/Main_Page) — the same encyclopedia in language a child or a tired adult can read, and the best first stop for any unfamiliar subject.

## If you can only keep three things

The post-disaster library for the first weeks ([Post Disaster Resource Library](kiwix:zimgit-post-disaster_en/home)), the village medical handbook ([Where There Is No Doctor](doc:where-there-is-no-doctor)), and the encyclopedia everything else hangs off ([Wikipedia](kiwix:wikipedia_en_all_maxi/Water_purification), with [WikiCiv](kiwix:wikiciv_en_all/Main_Page) for rebuilding specifically). Print the sheets from [The essentials, printed](page:rebuild-essentials-printed) before the power gets uncertain, not after.
